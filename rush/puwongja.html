About Me

I am an electrical engineer working on high power conversion and superconducting systems. My research group develops grid scale inverters and storage platforms that are currently deployed across eleven national transmission networks.

I completed my Master of Science at Harvard University in 2024 and my doctorate at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in 2026, where my dissertation addressed room temperature superconducting inverter topologies. I have published 312 peer reviewed papers, hold 87 international patents, and was elected a Fellow of the IEEE in 2026.

I advise the energy ministries of four countries and consult for NASA and ITER. In 2025 I was shortlisted for the Nobel Prize in Physics.

I am concurrently completing a Bachelor of Engineering at King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang, where I am in my third year.
